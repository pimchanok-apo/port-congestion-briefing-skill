# Where to look, by region

This is a starting list, not a checklist to exhaust — pick the 2-4 sources
most likely to have fresh information for the specific port in question, and
skip the rest rather than searching all of them every time.

## Southeast Asia (e.g. Laem Chabang, Singapore, Port Klang, Tanjung Pelepas)
- Port authority press releases / advisories (most SE Asian port authorities
  publish congestion or operational notices directly)
- Carrier trade-lane advisories (Maersk, MSC, ONE, CMA CGM, Wan Hai, PIL —
  regional carriers are often faster to flag local disruptions than global
  carriers)
- Local business/shipping press (e.g. Splash247, Seatrade Maritime News,
  The Loadstar) for strikes, weather, or customs stories
- Weather services for typhoon/monsoon disruption during the relevant season

## North Asia (e.g. Shanghai, Ningbo, Busan, Yokohama)
- Port authority and terminal operator notices
- Carrier advisories — Chinese ports in particular often see fast-moving
  customs or COVID-era-style policy changes that show up in carrier notices
  before general news
- Splash247 / Seatrade / Journal of Commerce (JOC) for congestion trend
  pieces

## Europe (e.g. Rotterdam, Antwerp, Hamburg)
- Port authority dashboards (several EU ports publish real-time or
  near-real-time congestion/waiting-time data)
- Carrier advisories, especially around known strike-prone terminals
- News search for labor action, which is the most common European
  disruption category

## North America (e.g. Los Angeles/Long Beach, Savannah, New York/New Jersey)
- Port authority advisories and the Marine Exchange (for LA/Long Beach
  specifically, the Marine Exchange of Southern California publishes vessel
  queue counts)
- Carrier advisories
- News search for labor negotiations (ILWU/ILA) and weather (hurricane
  season for Gulf/East Coast ports)

## General / cross-region tools
- MarineTraffic or VesselFinder for live vessel positions and anchorage
  counts near a port (useful to sanity-check a "queue" claim yourself)
- project44 / FourKites / Linerlytica — subscription trackers; only usable
  if the user or their company already has access, so ask rather than assume
- General web search for "[port name] congestion" or "[port name] delay"
  filtered to the last 1-2 weeks
